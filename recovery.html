The Anatomy of the Come-down

Understanding what's happening mechanically removes the anxiety. It's just chemistry, and chemistry can be balanced.

battery_0_bar

Severe Electrolyte Depletion

Dancing for 6 hours can cause you to sweat out up to 2 litres of fluid, carrying essential sodium, potassium, and magnesium with it. This creates micro-cramping and profound fatigue.

hub

Neurotransmitter Exhaustion

Your brain's reward centres have been flooded. The resulting 'empty' feeling isn't a moral failing; it's a literal lack of the chemical precursors needed to produce serotonin and dopamine.

mood_bad

Cortisol Dysregulation

Sleep deprivation spikes cortisol, worsening the emotional come-down. Magnesium and ashwagandha help regulate the HPA axis and bring stress hormones back to baseline faster.
